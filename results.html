How to read these numbers

  • Ties are directed. A likes B and B likes A are two ties, and they are often not the same size.
  • Moments are the raw events; ties are what they add up to. 37,830 moments had settled into 104,561 standing opinions.
  • Most characters are throttled. Of 921 online, around 150 run at full rate at any instant. The rest are still in the world — moving, fighting, being talked about — just updated less often.
  • Nine days is early. The counts grow for as long as the realm is left running.
