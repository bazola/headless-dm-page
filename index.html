The Headless DM dashboard

Headless DM is a private AzerothCore realm running around 900 playerbots that are driven by language models. They travel, fight, talk, form opinions about each other, join companies and keep memories of what happened to them.

The dashboard is the only window onto that. It is a single web page served by the world server that shows the whole realm live — where everyone is, what they feel, what they have done, and what has been written about it. This site explains how to read it.

Opening it

The world server serves the dashboard itself, on port 8787. On the machine running the realm:

http://127.0.0.1:8787/

Nothing to install and no login. To reach it from another machine on your own network, the server's Dashboard.Bind setting must include that machine's address.

Reading the dashboard needs no password. One thing is protected — pausing and resuming a character — and that needs a token, covered under Commands.

The realm trusts whoever can reach it. There are no per-user accounts. Anyone who can open the page can read everything on it, so bind it to your own machine or a private network, not the open internet.

What you are looking at

Every map on this site has its art switched off. The painted world map is extracted from the game client and is not ours to publish, so all screenshots here were taken with Layers ▸ Map art off — which leaves zone outlines, zone names, company holdings and live markers on a dark ground. On your own realm the painted map is on by default.
