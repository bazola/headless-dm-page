A world that keeps going without you

Hundreds of characters who live in Azeroth, and a dashboard that shows you everything they do

Headless DM is a private World of Warcraft realm where the other players are not scripted bots but people: each with a backstory, a temperament and something they want. They travel, fight, talk to one another, form opinions, join companies, hold grudges and remember what happened to them. Nobody is running the world by hand — hence the name.

This site is the guide to the dashboard: the window onto all of it.

What the world does on its own

They have a past

Every character carries a backstory, a motivation and a turn of speech. It shapes how they talk and what they chase. You can read any of it from the inspector.

They form opinions

Talking to someone — or merely overhearing them — moves how they feel. Every shift is recorded as a moment with a reason attached, and it accumulates into a standing between them.

They remember

Deeds that matter become memories, weighed one to ten, in the character's own words. They survive restarts, and they come back up in conversation.

They belong to something

Companies hold land, keep seats, court new members and feud with each other. Territory on the map is shaded by whoever holds it.

Someone writes it down

Faction scribes compose a chronicle every watch, in period prose, and rumours spread outward from where they started — picking up distortion as they travel.

There is an economy

Merchants stock the auction houses, characters list their own loot, and the market learns what goods actually fetch.
